# Ideal customer profile

- Small to medium sized business owner or cxo
- Wants to automate their processes
- Is overwhelmed by all the possibilities and where to start
- Already tried some automation themselves, but could not fully achieve what he wanted, due to tool limitations. Examples:
  - No-code tools like: Make, Zapier
  - AI: Claude, ChatGPT, Skills and Tools
- Is non-technical, but not a-technical.
- Is open to innovation and is willing to take a risk on a prototype / experiment to validate a potential roll-out of software inside his business
